Our story

A tiny oven, a big obsession.

Brindle started with one stubborn sourdough starter and a borrowed oven. Today we bake for the whole neighborhood — but the method hasn't changed: real ingredients, long fermentation, and no shortcuts.

Everything leaving our kitchen is made the same morning you buy it. What doesn't sell goes to the community fridge down the street.
